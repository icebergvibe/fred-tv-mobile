# Fred TV Mobile

Fred TV for Android and iOS

> **This is a fork.** The app is [Fred TV Mobile](https://github.com/Fredolx/fred-tv-mobile)
> by [Fredolx](https://github.com/Fredolx), who wrote and maintains it (see also
> [Fred TV](https://github.com/Fredolx/open-tv) for desktop). All credit for the app goes to
> them - the store links and donation options below are the original author's, and they are
> the right place to support this project.
>
> The `chromecast` branch of this fork adds **Google Cast (Chromecast) support to the Android
> app**: a cast button in the player and toolbar, a device picker, remote controls, and a
> phone-side pipeline that remuxes or transcodes any IPTV stream to H.264/AAC HLS with FFmpeg
> and serves it to the Chromecast from the phone. The phone stays the provider's only client,
> so it works with the single-connection limit most IPTV providers enforce. Everything else is
> unchanged; iOS is untouched.
>
> No binaries are published from this fork. Build it yourself (see
> [CHROMECAST.md](CHROMECAST.md) for the design notes and build steps), or wait for the
> feature to land upstream.
